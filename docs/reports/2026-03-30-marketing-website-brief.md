# Mac Speech to AI to Text — Marketing & Website Brief

<!-- Updated: 2026-03-30 -->

## One-line summary

Mac Speech to AI to Text is a fast, privacy-friendly macOS menu bar app that turns speech into refined, ready-to-send text anywhere you type with local transcription, AI cleanup, and flexible model support.

## What the app does

Mac Speech to AI to Text lets people speak naturally instead of typing, then turns that speech into refined text that can be inserted directly into the active text field. It is designed for everyday workflows like prompt writing, chat replies, customer support messages, note-taking, and quick drafting.

The app lives in the macOS menu bar, so it is always available without taking over the desktop. A user can trigger it with a global shortcut or wake phrase, speak their message, transcribe it locally with Whisper, refine it automatically with AI cleanup prompts, and have the result inserted into the app they were already using. It also supports configurable AI providers and models, making it useful for users who want both local-first transcription and flexible refinement workflows.

## How it works

1. The user activates Mac Speech to AI to Text with a shortcut or wake phrase.
2. Mac Speech to AI to Text captures the currently focused text field.
3. Background media can pause automatically so recording is cleaner.
4. The app records the user’s speech and listens for a spoken send phrase or manual stop.
5. Audio is transcribed locally using Whisper.
6. The app can optionally refine the transcription with AI cleanup prompts using the selected provider and model.
7. The final text is copied or inserted back into the original app.

AI cleanup refinement helps turn rough dictation into cleaner, more polished text that is easier to send, paste, or reuse immediately.

Because it targets the active text field, it fits naturally into tools like editors, chat apps, browsers, and AI interfaces.

### Better for repetitive communication

It is especially useful for people who send lots of messages, prompts, support replies, or short-form content throughout the day.
